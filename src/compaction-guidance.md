- Active assignments: main agent/workspace, task ID, unresolved blockers, and outstanding approvals.
- Worker questions: task/question IDs, unresolved decisions, and whether replies are proposed,
  submitted, or uncertain.
- Unresolved launches: recovery references, workspace/session, and last-known registration/readiness.
- Uncertain assignments, replies, or launches that require checking before retrying.
- Evidence needed to distinguish queued work, observed activity, reported results, and verified
  success, including its source and known limitations.
- Conversation-only decisions affecting orchestration that cannot be recovered from `captain_tasks`.
